    Practice tool for learning and friendly games. "Pot now" includes any bet you're facing. Leave "To call" at 0 if nobody has bet.

    How the coach decides
    • It deals out thousands of random finishes against your number of opponents and counts how often you win. That's your equity.
    • Average is your fair share: 1 ÷ number of players. Beating it by a good margin means you're ahead.
    • Pot odds: to call 20 into a pot of 100, you need to win 20 ÷ 120 ≈ 17% of the time to break even.
    • Strong equity → Raise. Enough to beat pot odds → Call. Nothing to call → Check. Otherwise → Fold.
    • Rule of 4 and 2: on the flop, outs × 4 ≈ % to hit by the river. On the turn, outs × 2.
