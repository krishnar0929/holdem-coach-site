    How the recommendation is calculated
    • Preflop decisions follow a starting-hand chart ranked from millions of simulated hands, adjusted for position and for raises in front of you.
    • After the flop, equity is measured against the hands opponents are likely to hold given how they bet, not against random cards. A bet narrows a range toward strong hands and draws.
    • Pot odds = to call ÷ (pot + to call). A call is profitable when equity beats this price. The expected value of the call is shown in chips.
    • Value bets and raises are sized at ⅔ to ¾ of the pot. Strong draws are played as semi-bluffs.
    • Outs use the rule of 4 and 2: outs × 4 on the flop, outs × 2 on the turn.
